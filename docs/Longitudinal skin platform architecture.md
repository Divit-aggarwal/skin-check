# Longitudinal Skin Platform: Measure Less, Compare Honestly

**Bottom line.** The simplest credible architecture is one spine, not twelve components: guided capture → quality and comparability gate → the existing CV engine emitting per-region ordinal scores with a measured noise floor → an append-only, engine-versioned skin-state log → a comparison layer that outputs evidence tiers rather than percent changes → a deterministic, versioned rule set over the brand's catalog → a compact fact package that Gemini only renders. Digital Twin, Forecasting, a separate Routine Engine and ML recommendation are a rename, a projection over the log, or research-dependent. The hard problems are measurement, not AI: phone colour error of ΔE00 ≈ 10–13 against a reference dwarfs any plausible routine effect, smartphone redness agrees with clinical imaging at ICC 0.15–0.24, run-chart persistence tests need ≥10 points, and measured topical adherence falls from 84.6% to 51% in eight weeks. The decisions that matter are in section 15; the most consequential is a test–retest study on the existing engine before any longitudinal claim reaches a user.

## 1. Executive Understanding

The product is a **longitudinal skin-state record with a noise model**, narrated in cosmetic language, and it should be designed as a measurement instrument first and an AI product second. Its job is to answer three user questions honestly: has anything measurable changed beyond scan-to-scan noise, what else changed in the same window, and what does the brand's rule set say to do next. Prediction, diagnosis and causal attribution are out of scope by regulation, by Gemini's own terms, and by the statistics of five to ten noisy observations.

**One-line model:** *scan → quality/comparability gate → per-region phenotype with uncertainty → append-only state (engine-versioned) → change vs noise floor and persistence → deterministic rules with audit → fact package → Gemini renders, never computes.*

In the digital-twin field's own taxonomy this is a "digital shadow" (one-way data flow from the physical system) over a longitudinal user model, not a twin, because it has no validated predictive model and no closed, verified feedback loop [Tudor, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01910-w). That framing is a strength: a shadow is cheaper, defensible to regulators, and matches what the data can support.

## 2. Reality Check

The proposed twelve-component architecture is directionally right about separation of concerns but wrong about where the difficulty lives: it under-invests in measurement reliability and capture control and over-invests in intelligence layers that the evidence cannot yet feed.

### What is strong

Separating Measurement, Skin State and Longitudinal Intelligence mirrors measurement science: COSMIN treats measurement error as a property of the instrument and sets SDC = 1.96 × √2 × SEM as the smallest detectable change [COSMIN, 2024](https://www.cosmin.nl/wp-content/uploads/COSMIN-manual-V2_final.pdf), and de Vet separates that noise floor (MDC) from the importance floor (MIC) [de Vet, 2006](https://research.vu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/2884976/263493.pdf). Image Comparability as its own concern is correct: clinical photography achieves serial comparability by fixing geometry and lighting and recording settings, not by post-hoc correction [IMI Wound Management, 2019](https://www.imi.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/2019_Apr_IMINatGuidelines_Wound-Management.pdf), and DICOM Supplement 221 is the only formal standard modelling serial skin imaging with tracking identifiers and a frame of reference [DICOM Sup 221, 2021](https://www.dicomstandard.org/News-dir/ftsup/docs/sups/sup221.pdf).

Gemini as a layer rather than the brain is supported by attribution research: generating from supplied evidence scored 65.5 on AIS versus 55.6 for post-hoc justification and 46.0 with no retrieval [Bohnet, 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.08037). Adherence Tracking is justified because without it trends are uninterpretable: MEMS-measured topical adherence fell from 84.6% to 51% over eight weeks while diaries overestimated use [Carroll, 2004](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0190962204005547).

### What is weak

The Measurement Layer implicitly trusts the engine. No peer-reviewed test–retest data exists for any consumer face-analysis app; the only repeatability figures come from a clinical rig (VISIA absolute wrinkle score SD ≈ 3%, percentile SD ≈ 9–11%) [Henseler, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10665717/) and one smartphone colorimetry app whose redness channel agreed with VISIA at ICC 0.15–0.24 [Ouyang, 2026](https://www.dovepress.com/bridging-accessibility-and-precision-evaluating-the-reliability-and-va-peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-CCID). Comedone detection on smartphone photos reached only mAP 0.40 [Huynh, 2022](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4418/12/8/1879).

Image Comparability framed as normalization is weak because medical-imaging harmonisation does not transfer: ComBat needs many samples per known batch, while a consumer session is one image with lighting perfectly confounded with the user's skin [Sci Rep ComBat, 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-23328-0). Longitudinal Intelligence on sparse scans is weak because persistence heuristics require more than 10 points and the five-point trend rule "has been shown to be at best useless" [Anhøj, 2015](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0121349).

Product Knowledge built on ingredient-interaction rules is thinner than it looks: the one chemically verified incompatibility is tretinoin degrading with benzoyl peroxide under light, which does not apply to adapalene [Martin, 1998](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Chemical-stability-of-adapalene-and-tretinoin-when-Martin-Meunier/2702ea48f3fbe746c0b307f735a205fff3c3473d); the niacinamide–vitamin C incompatibility and copper-peptide rules had no peer-reviewed support in the notes, and neither did "thinnest to thickest" ordering or skin cycling.

### What is unnecessarily complicated

"Digital Twin" is a label, not a component: 18 of 149 self-described human digital twins (12.08%) met the NASEM criteria and only two mentioned verification, validation and uncertainty quantification [Tudor, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01910-w). "Forecasting" has no evidentiary floor: full multivariable models explained 22% (men) and 37.2% (women) of wrinkle variation in a 3,831-person cohort, with heritability near 55% [Hamer, 2020](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/126158/proefschrift-facial-skin-aging-merel-hamer.pdf), and the only large Indian reference set is cross-sectional and "cannot infer individual skin aging trajectories" [Reddy, 2026](https://www.cureus.com/articles/465674-artificial-intelligence-driven-skin-parameter-assessment-in-indian-adults-establishing-population-specific-reference-data-for-clinical-dermatology).

Product Knowledge, Routine Engine and Recommendation Engine collapse into one constraint-based recommender over a versioned catalog: Felfernig's model already has customer variables, product variables, compatibility constraints, filter constraints and the catalog as its parts, plus utility ranking of survivors [Felfernig et al.](https://www.ise.bgu.ac.il/faculty/liorr/recsyshb/chConstraint.pdf). The Dashboard is a set of projections over the event log with evidence tiers attached, not a component with its own logic.

## 3. Recommended System Architecture

The architecture is a single deterministic pipeline with one append-only system of record, where probabilistic components (the CV engine, change tests) emit values with uncertainty and the LLM sits strictly downstream as a renderer.

### Conceptual data flow

```
PHONE (guided capture; on-device pose / expression / exposure / distance checks)
  │  original image + capture metadata (device, camera, flash, exposure, pose, timestamp)
  ▼
INGEST  presigned upload → malware scan → validate + re-encode (EXIF to sidecar, GPS dropped)
  │
  ▼
QUALITY GATE ──fail──▶ immediate retake prompt with one named reason
  │  pass / pass-with-caveats  →  QualityReport (per check, per region, model version)
  ▼
MEASUREMENT  existing CV engine, region-wise (forehead, L/R cheek, nose, chin, periorbital)
  │  per-region ordinal grades, lesion counts, CIELAB/ITA, per-metric interval, engine version
  ▼
SKIN STATE  one immutable document per scan per engine version (never updated in place)
  │
  ▼
COMPARABILITY  pairwise, per region: comparable / comparable-with-caveats / not comparable
  │  caveats: device change, flash state, lighting asymmetry, pose, expression, occlusion
  ▼
LONGITUDINAL COMPARE  noise floor (MDC95 / RCI) → importance floor → persistence (≥10 pts)
  │  → evidence tier 0–4, with alternative explanations (cycle, season, settling, adherence)
  ▼
RECOMMENDATION RULES  red-flag interlock → eligibility (Unique) → slot/schedule → interactions
  │  → ranking; emits fired rules, knowledge sources, rule-set version, product version
  ▼
CONTEXT PACKAGE  deterministic rendering: fact sentences with stable IDs, pre-templated numbers,
  │  explicit unknowns, allowed-claim taxonomy
  ▼
GEMINI  schema-forced output (each claim cites a state ID) → validator → NLI post-check → UI
                                                                     └─ fallback template on fail

═══════════════ SYSTEM OF RECORD: PostgreSQL in ap-south-1, insert-only event log ═══════════════
 ScanCompleted · QualityReport · SkinState(engine version) · Comparability · RoutineEvent ·
 ProductUseEvent · ContextEvent · UserAssertion(status) · RecommendationIssued(rules, versions) ·
 RedFlagEvent · ChatTurn(package hash, template version) · ConsentRecord
 derived projections (recomputable): current routine · adherence summary · trend summary · package
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### How the pieces interact

Capture is where comparability is won or lost, so the on-device gates (single face, pose within tolerance, neutral expression, face-box size as distance proxy, exposure statistics on skin pixels, texture-fidelity blur score) run before upload; MediaPipe exposes 478 landmarks, 52 blendshapes and a canonical-face transformation matrix on Android, iOS and Web [MediaPipe Face Landmarker](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker), and a skin micro-relief gradability model runs in about 20 ms on an iPhone 12 mini [Beyond Blur, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03095). Landmarks are transient and never persisted (section 8).

The pipeline is event-driven and idempotent: a Standard Step Functions execution named by scan identifier gives exactly-once start and a 90-day inspectable history [Step Functions workflow types](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/latest/dg/choosing-workflow-type.html), and every consumer tolerates duplicates because S3, EventBridge, SQS and Lambda are at-least-once [Lambda with SQS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lambda/latest/dg/with-sqs.html). Images never pass through Next.js (1 MB server-action body limit) [Next.js serverActions config, 2026-09-07](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/config/next-config-js/serverActions); the server only mints short-lived presigned URLs and reads projections through a data-access layer that enforces ownership [Next.js data security, 2026-08-25](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/data-security).

Routine, adherence and recommendation history are event-sourced because they are the explanatory variables for every trend; an overwritten routine row destroys the timeline the comparison layer needs [AWS event sourcing pattern](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/event-sourcing.html). Design choice: stay on PostgreSQL/Supabase (ap-south-1 available [Supabase regions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions)) with skin state as immutable JSONB documents rather than adding DynamoDB, which serves queries "in a limited number of ways" [DynamoDB NoSQL design](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/bp-general-nosql-design.html).

### Trust boundary

| Zone | Contents | Who may write | Shown to the user as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic / system of record | Event log, catalog and product versions, rule set and fired rules, QualityReport, Comparability verdict, all computed statistics (deltas, MDC, adherence ratios), ConsentRecord | Application services only, via deterministic actions | Facts, with source and date |
| Probabilistic, uncertainty-bearing | CV engine outputs and intervals, QC classifier scores, change tests, evidence tiers | Measurement and comparison services; always tagged with engine/model version | Observations with a stated noise band and evidence tier |
| LLM-rendered | Natural-language explanation of facts already in the package; proposed (unconfirmed) UserAssertion candidates | Gemini, through a schema; never writes to the record | Explanation that cites the facts it used |

The LLM never computes a number, never writes measured or logged state, and never decides a recommendation; this is the operational meaning of "Gemini is not magic" and it follows Anthropic's workflow-over-agent guidance for bounded tasks [Anthropic, Building effective agents, 2024](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents).

## 4. Core Data Model

The model should be an append-only observation timeline with explicit provenance per fact and versions on everything that can change meaning, replacing the linear User→Scan→SkinState→Trend→Routine→ProductUsage→Recommendation→Conversation chain with a log plus derived projections.

Three sources dictate the shape: Open mHealth's data-point header carries a provenance modality of "sensed" or "self-reported" per observation [Open mHealth header schema](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openmhealth/schemas/master/schema/omh/header-1.1.json); Kobsa's user-modelling services require assumptions "as well as justifications for these assumptions" and separate explicit from implicit information [Kobsa, 2001](https://ics.uci.edu/~kobsa/papers/2001-UMUAI-kobsa.pdf); and EHR deep learning scaled on a time-ordered event stream without upfront harmonisation [Rajkomar, 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-018-0029-1). Design choice: extend the modality enum to sensed / self-reported / inferred / confirmed.

Three structural rules follow. SkinState is never updated: an engine upgrade produces new rows, and a trend is drawn only through rows of the same engine version or rows explicitly re-processed, the consumer analogue of RECIST's "same technique at baseline and follow-up" [RECIST 1.1, 2009](https://project.eortc.org/recist/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2015/03/RECISTGuidelines.pdf). An absent ProductUseEvent is unknown, not a missed application, because self-report already overstates use [Carroll, 2004](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0190962204005547). Deletion operates at the user level (crypto-shred or hard-delete all of a user's events), never by editing events, which reconciles append-only storage with DPDP erasure duties [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).

Provenance is split from audit as FHIR does: Provenance records how a fact came to be, AuditEvent who used it [FHIR Provenance](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/provenance.html). Design choice: no "Condition" entity at all, because drawing conclusions "about a person's health status or health risk (irrespective of whether these conclusions are accurate or inaccurate)" creates health data [WP29 Annex, 2015](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/article-29/documentation/other-document/files/2015/20150205_letter_art29wp_ec_health_data_after_plenary_annex_en.pdf).

## 5. Measurement Layer

The existing engine must be re-specified to emit per-region ordinal grades and integer counts with an engine version, quality flags and an empirically measured noise floor, and anything it currently reports as hydration, sebum or a precise redness index must be demoted to a labelled proxy or removed.

### What the engine must output per scan

| Output | Form | Why this form | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acne | Inflammatory and non-inflammatory lesion counts per zone (forehead, each cheek, nose, chin), plus a derived ordinal 0–4 grade | FDA defines the IGA as an ordinal 0–4 with morphological descriptions and requires counts reported separately; GAGS scores six zones | [FDA Acne Guidance, 2018](https://www.fda.gov/files/drugs/published/Acne-Vulgaris---Establishing-Effectiveness-of-Drugs-Intended-for-Treatment.pdf); [Ann Dermatol, 2023](https://anndermatol.org/DOIx.php?id=10.5021%2Fad.23.094) |
| Pigmentation | Per-region area × darkness (mMASI style) over forehead, left/right malar, chin weighted 3:3:3:1; no "homogeneity/evenness" score in change claims | Homogeneity was the least reliable component even for expert raters and was dropped from mMASI | [Pandya, 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20398960/) |
| Colour | CIELAB L*, a*, b* per region after device colour correction; ITA as the within-user relative covariate, never an absolute tone category from one photo | Reference ITA bands are 13–18° wide and exposure/flash alone shifts phone ITA by tens of degrees | [Ly, 2020](https://www.jidonline.org/article/S0022-202X(19)33397-4/fulltext); [arXiv 2411.13832, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.13832v1) |
| Wrinkles | Per-site 5-point photonumeric grade; one grade is the smallest meaningful unit | Human inter-rater ICC 0.736–0.845; a 1-point difference was established as clinically significant | [Lorenc, 2021](https://academic.oup.com/asjopenforum/article/3/4/ojab039/6407293) |
| Redness | Ordinal 0–4, low-confidence tier, ±1 grade treated as noise | 12 dermatologists matched the panel mean 71.1% of the time and differed by one grade 28% of the time | [Tan via Healio, 2014](https://www.healio.com/news/dermatology/20141003/cea-reliable-for-assessment-of-facial-erythema-of-rosacea) |
| Pores | Relative ordinal visibility score, not count or micrometre size | Pore quantification correlated r=0.76 with experts only under 34× polarised magnification | [Cosmetic Dermatology, 2009](https://cdn.mdedge.com/files/s3fs-public/Document/September-2017/022090457.pdf) |
| Per-metric uncertainty | Interval or ±SEM per region from the calibration table, widened by capture-quality bucket | ICC alone hides a large SEM; report SEM in native units | [COSMIN, 2024](https://www.cosmin.nl/wp-content/uploads/COSMIN-manual-V2_final.pdf) |
| Engine version and quality flags | Engine version, QC model version, per-region visibility fraction, out-of-scope flags | Users must receive performance for "appropriate subgroups… acceptable inputs, known limitations" | [GMLP, 2021](https://www.fda.gov/media/153486/download) |

Absolute scores, never percentiles: VISIA's wrinkle absolute score had SD ≈ 3% across repeated captures while its percentile score had SD ≈ 9–11%, and the authors concluded absolute scores "should be preferred over percentiles owing to their better precision" [Henseler, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10665717/). Region-wise output also solves occlusion: a hair-covered forehead becomes a missing region, not a corrupted whole-face score.

### What phone photos cannot credibly measure

Sebum (Sebumeter films), hydration (Corneometer capacitance), elasticity and surface roughness come from contact instruments whose physics an RGB camera does not reproduce [Maia Campos, 2019](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2019.00254/full); melanin and erythema indices come from narrow-band LED reflectance under standardised illuminant and geometry [Ly, 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022202X19333974). Smartphone pigmentation metrics can be valid (L* r 0.71–0.77, ITA r −0.81 to −0.87 against a spectrophotometer) while smartphone redness is not (ICC 0.15–0.24 against VISIA), attributed to insufficient red spectral content in phone LEDs [Ouyang, 2026](https://www.dovepress.com/bridging-accessibility-and-precision-evaluating-the-reliability-and-va-peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-CCID). Any engine output carrying an instrument's name is a learned proxy and must be labelled as such; "oiliness" from a photo is at best a specular-shine proxy.

### Uncertainty and quality requirements the team must produce itself

No published SEM or MDC exists for any photo-derived skin metric, so the noise model is the team's first research deliverable. The supported design is a same-person, same-day repeated-capture study: at least 30 users spanning Monk Skin Tone bins 4–9, at least 3 captures each under two lighting conditions, with a second session four weeks later following the only published template [Henseler, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10665717/); splits by user, not by image [GMLP, 2021](https://www.fda.gov/media/153486/download).

Statistics per metric × region × tone band: ICC(2,1) two-way random absolute agreement with 95% CI, read against the Koo & Li bands (<0.50 poor, 0.50–0.75 moderate, 0.75–0.90 good, >0.90 excellent) [Koo & Li via easystats](https://easystats.github.io/effectsize/reference/interpret_icc.html) (primary unverified); SEM in native units; MDC95 = 1.96 × √2 × SEM; weighted kappa and "same grade / ±1 grade" probabilities for ordinal outputs; Bland–Altman limits against dermatologist grades rather than Pearson r [COSMIN, 2024](https://www.cosmin.nl/wp-content/uploads/COSMIN-manual-V2_final.pdf). COSMIN's sufficiency criterion is ≥0.70; realistic targets from the one smartphone validation are ICC ≥0.85 for pigmentation and ≥0.70 elsewhere [Ouyang, 2026](https://www.dovepress.com/bridging-accessibility-and-precision-evaluating-the-reliability-and-va-peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-CCID).

Stratification must be by ITA/Monk, not Fitzpatrick: image-derived ITA agreed with dermatologists' Fitzpatrick ratings 20% of the time on faces but 89–92% with Monk categories [Ulrich, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01770-4), and expert Monk labels carry a ±1-bin floor with Indian annotators rating skin lighter than US annotators [Schumann, 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.09073v3). Design choice: pool into MST 1–3, 4–6 and 7–10 and power the validation set within the darker half, because on the only pathology-confirmed diverse benchmark model sensitivity fell from 0.41 to 0.12 and 0.69 to 0.23 between Fitzpatrick I–II and V–VI, and dermatologists fell from 0.72 to 0.59 [Daneshjou, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9374341/).

For an engine the team cannot retrain, split conformal prediction gives distribution-free intervals from a calibration set alone [Angelopoulos & Bates, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511); temperature scaling calibrates any exposed classifier head with one scalar [Guo, 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599). Exchangeability fails if calibration images are clinic-lit and production images are Indian homes, so calibration data must come from the production population.

## 6. Longitudinal Intelligence

Comparison must be built as a gate sequence (comparable? → beyond noise? → beyond importance? → persistent?) that emits a bounded evidence tier with named alternative explanations, and the honest output below roughly ten comparable points is "not enough evidence," not a trend.

### Noise floor, importance floor, and why naive percent change fails

Two thresholds per metric are mandatory. The noise floor is the engine's own MDC95, equivalently the Reliable Change Index: standardised change beyond 1.96 exceeds "simple measurement unreliability" [Blampied, 2022](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/the-cognitive-behaviour-therapist/article/reliable-change-and-the-reliable-change-index-still-useful-after-all-these-years/75AC9F09E738AC53924DA77AE4C66D7D); the importance floor is anchor-based and, absent the brand's own anchor study, must be set conservatively and labelled provisional [de Vet, 2006](https://research.vu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/2884976/263493.pdf). Published MCIDs do not transfer: EASI 6.6, SCORAD 8.7 and POEM 3.4 are clinician-scored disease instruments, and clinician IGA intra-rater ICC is only 0.54 [CADTH, 2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK565237/); an anchor-based mMASI MCID exists but its value was not retrievable [Rodrigues, 2016](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamadermatology/fullarticle/2519450) (unverified).

Percent change fails three ways. Denominator: FDA recommends absolute lesion-count change as primary and percent as secondary [FDA Acne Guidance, 2018](https://www.fda.gov/media/71152/download). Responder: adapalene monotherapy produced a 50.82% mean lesion reduction yet only 7.74% of patients met the two-grade IGA criterion at week 12 [Luan, 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13555-024-01286-x). Baseline: extreme first values regress toward the mean, more so with larger measurement error and selection on baseline [Barnett, 2005](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/34/1/215/638499), so the baseline is a window (median of the first three comparable scans or first 14 days), not scan one.

### Persistence rules and minimum points

Run-chart shift and run rules "require more than 10 points before they are applicable" [Perla, 2011](https://semcme.org/wp-content/uploads/The-Run-Chart-Perla-R-the-BMJ-2011-1.pdf). Anhøj's two tests (longest run > round(log2(n) + 3); crossings < the 5% binomial quantile of n − 1) apply "with as few as 10" points and reach LR+ ≈ 10 with 12+ points, versus LR+ 2–4 for the Perla set [qicharts2 vignette](https://anhoej.github.io/qicharts2/articles/qicharts2.html); [Anhøj, 2015](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0121349). The five-point trend rule must never ship.

Design choice for the gate: fewer than 4 comparable points → "baseline forming"; 4–9 → direction only with the noise band; ≥10 → Anhøj tests. For 3–10 irregular points a Theil–Sen median-of-slopes estimator with a nonparametric interval is more robust than least squares [scikit-learn robust estimators](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/linear_model.html); a Kalman local-level smoother on a daily grid with missing days widens uncertainty across gaps [statsmodels state space](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/statespace.html). Bayesian online change-point detection is a hypothesis generator for aligning level shifts with product-start dates, not a verdict, because at ≤10 points the prior dominates [Adams & MacKay, 2007](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742).

### Natural fluctuation that the comparison must condition on

Untreated variation is of the same order as early product effects: inflammatory lesion counts rose 25.3% from follicular to late-luteal phase in 25 women over two untreated cycles [Lucky, 2004](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamadermatology/fullarticle/480456), 44% of 400 women reported premenstrual worsening [Stoll, 2001](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S019096220123641X), exam stress correlated r=0.61 with acne severity after adjusting for sleep and diet [Chiu, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1001/archderm.139.7.897), and hydration, sebum and brightness tracked monthly temperature and humidity over 13 months in 89 women [Nam, 2015](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/srt.12145). These come from clinician counts and contact instruments, so they are priors for the existence and sign of fluctuation, not calibration values; no India-specific seasonal data were found.

Consequences: an acne-type trend needs a window covering one full cycle with like-phase comparison for menstruating users who opt in; season boundaries (pre-monsoon to monsoon) are stored as ContextEvents and surfaced as alternative explanations; one-item stress and sleep check-ins at scan time enable the "concurrent change" logic.

### Regional analysis and comparability gating

Regions are the unit of comparison: anatomical region explained more colour variance than device (η² 0.18 vs 0.12) and region-specific colour correction raised cross-device ICC from 0.78–0.80 to 0.93–0.95 [arXiv 2512.21988, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21988). The zone set is MASI's forehead, left and right malar and chin for pigmentation [Pandya, 2011](https://www.jaad.org/article/S0190-9622(09)02302-0/fulltext) and GAGS's forehead, cheeks, nose and chin for acne [IJDVL acne scoring](https://ijdvl.com/scoring-systems-in-acne-vulgaris/); periorbital is an engineering addition without a fetched scale.

Comparability is a three-tier verdict per region pair, mirroring RECIST's "not evaluable" and the trial-photography category "unable to be determined" (under a controlled protocol 89.9% of images were adequate, 8.4% suboptimal-but-usable and 1.7% uninterpretable) [PLoS ONE, 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0110395): Comparable; Comparable with caveats (device, flash state, lighting asymmetry, pose); Not comparable (dominant cause named, retake offered). "Not comparable" suppresses numeric change for affected metrics but still shows images side by side, as Miiskin does [Miiskin](https://miiskin.com/about/news/press-release-automatic-skin-imaging/). Colour-sensitive comparisons require the same camera and flash state unless a per-device colour-correction matrix exists, because flash on/off produced hysteresis-like ITA shifts and overhead lights "created significant ITA variability" [arXiv 2411.13832, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.13832v1).

### Evidence tiers and what each needs

| Tier | Label to user | Entry condition | What raises it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Too early | Inside the active's expected-effect window, or <4 comparable scans | More scheduled scans |
| 1 | Observation | Change beyond MDC95 on ≥4 comparable scans; no persistence test | Consistent capture conditions |
| 2 | Pattern | Anhøj test passed on ≥10 comparable points, or Theil–Sen window change > MDC95 with interval excluding zero | Cycle-phase matching; fewer gaps |
| 3 | Association, with alternatives | Tier 2 + product start precedes change + implementation ≥ ~70% and coverage ≥ 50% + listed concurrent changes | Fewer concurrent product changes |
| 4 | Tested | Only via opt-in on/off protocol for fast-onset/offset products (hydration, surface shine); never retinoids, acids or pigment actives | — |

The ladder is an inference from GRADE (non-randomised evidence starts at "low" certainty) [Cochrane Handbook ch. 14](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14) and Evidence DEFINED's digital caveats on attrition and selective reporting [Silberman, 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-023-00836-5); GRADE terms never appear in the UI. Tier 4 is bounded by AHRQ's N-of-1 criteria (stable condition, rapid onset and offset, washout, randomised crossovers), which slow actives violate [AHRQ N-of-1 guide, 2014](https://effectivehealthcare.ahrq.gov/products/n-1-trials/research-2014-5). Only temporality is checked mechanically; Hill's other viewpoints are "considerations," not a checklist [Shimonovich, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8206235/).

### Informative sampling and re-baselining

Self-initiated scans are the textbook informative-visit problem: ignoring outcome-dependent visit timing produced a two-fold over-estimate of prevalence in the cited clinic example [IrregLong vignette](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/IrregLong/vignettes/Irreglong-vignette.html). The CORE response is design, not statistics: a weekly scheduled anchor scan via a fixed-time reminder (DentalMonitoring's pattern [DentalMonitoring IFU, 2023](https://dentalmonitoring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/IFU_MON_P_MD_DentalMonitoring-Patient-Instructions-For-Use_01_en.pdf)), every scan tagged scheduled or ad hoc, and coverage shown ("8 scans over 6 weeks, 5 scheduled"). Inverse-intensity weighting and joint visit-process models need many users and are LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT.

Engine change triggers re-baselining: re-process retained originals under the new version for consenting users, or mark a version boundary and compute trends only within version with cross-boundary comparisons shown as uncertain; running both engines on new scans during a transition lets per-user offsets be estimated. Missing scans are plausibly missing-not-at-random (users skip when breaking out), so conclusions should be checked under a pessimistic scenario before any "improving" message; mixed-effects models and LMM-based imputation for irregular spacing [Huque, 2018](https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12874-018-0615-6) are LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT.

## 7. Recommendation Architecture

Recommendation should be a deterministic constraint-satisfaction recommender over a versioned own-brand catalog, authored as decision tables with explicit hit policies and a fired-rule audit log, with the LLM rendering pre-computed explanations and any ML ranking deferred until interaction data and propensity logs exist.

### Paradigm

Knowledge-based recommenders "exploit predefined recommender knowledge bases that contain explicit rules" and have "no cold-start problems since requirements are directly elicited within a recommendation session" [Felfernig et al.](https://www.ise.bgu.ac.il/faculty/liorr/recsyshb/chConstraint.pdf). The mapping is direct: compatibility constraints are user-state rules (pregnancy × retinoid), filter constraints are product eligibility for a skin state and goal, product constraints are the catalog, and multi-attribute utility ranks survivors. When nothing satisfies the constraints, Felfernig's minimal-conflict and repair machinery names the blocking requirement; only preference constraints may be relaxed automatically, never safety constraints. Collaborative filtering is wrong early: sparsity with a small own catalog [Burke, 2002](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1021240730564), no representation of contraindications, and "users like you also bought" is not a defensible explanation for a product applied to skin.

### Rule authoring and governance

Rules are versioned data in DMN decision tables (DMN 1.5, August 2024 [OMG DMN](https://www.omg.org/spec/DMN/1.5)) or an equivalent JSON rules format [json-rules-engine](https://github.com/CacheControl/json-rules-engine). Hit policies make conflicts explicit: Unique (overlap is an authoring error), Any, Priority, First, Collect [Red Hat DMN docs](https://docs.redhat.com/en/documentation/red_hat_process_automation_manager/7.2/html/designing_a_decision_service_using_dmn_models/dmn-con_dmn-models). Design choice: Table A eligibility gate (Unique) → Table B slot and schedule (Priority) → Table C interaction check over ingredient-class pairs (Collect) → Table D scoring weights (Collect/sum). Rules attach to ingredient classes, never SKUs, so a new SKU inherits them through its INCI list; SKU overrides may only add caution.

Every Recommendation stores rule-set version, product version identifiers, an input-snapshot hash, fired rules with knowledge sources, and ranking scores, so identical inputs reproduce the output and the audit trail doubles as ASCI's substantiation with source and date [ASCI Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code/).

### Evidence-based rules versus folklore

Expected-effect windows belong in the knowledge base so the system can say "too early": tretinoin's label states results "after two to three weeks" and "more than six weeks" for definite benefit, with early exacerbation that "should not be considered a reason to discontinue" [RETIN-A label, 2002](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2002/16921s21s22s25lbl.pdf); niacinamide pigmentation effects were detectable at 4 weeks in an 8-week trial [Boo, 2021](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3921/10/8/1315); azelaic acid improved at 6 weeks in an Indian cohort [Iraji, 2007](https://ijdvl.com/efficacy-of-topical-azelaic-acid-gel-in-the-treatment-of-mild-moderate-acne-vulgaris/); BPO 2.5% reduced P. acnes within 2 weeks and equalled 5% and 10% on inflammatory lesions [Mills, 1986](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2948929/); AHAs for photodamage ran 22 weeks [Stiller, 1996](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamadermatology/article-abstract/557798); vitamin C 12–24 weeks [Al-Niaimi, 2017](https://jcadonline.com/topical-vitamin-c-and-the-skin-mechanisms-of-action-and-clinical-applications/). These are prescription-strength figures under trial adherence: earliest plausible windows, not effect sizes. Vehicle arms improved 19.9–40% on their own [Iraji, 2007](https://ijdvl.com/efficacy-of-topical-azelaic-acid-gel-in-the-treatment-of-mild-moderate-acne-vulgaris/); [Stiller, 1996](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamadermatology/article-abstract/557798), the core reason the system cannot attribute improvement to a product.

### Routine model

FHIR's Dosage and Timing datatypes supply the RoutineItem vocabulary: sequence, frequency/period/periodUnit, dayOfWeek, timeOfDay, event codes such as MORN, EVE and HS, bounds and count, and additional instructions for warnings [FHIR R4 Dosage](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/dosage.html); [FHIR R4 Timing](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/datatypes.html#Timing). MedicationStatement's status set (active, intended, on-hold, stopped, completed, not-taken) and its "reported, not verified" semantics are the template for routine items and self-reported adherence [FHIR R4 MedicationStatement](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/medicationstatement.html). The full CarePlan graph, with practitioners, encounters and orders, does not transfer and would mis-frame a cosmetic product [FHIR R4 CarePlan](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/careplan.html).

Adherence follows the ABC taxonomy (initiation, implementation, discontinuation/persistence) [Vrijens, 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3403197/): implementation is used days over logged days, coverage is logged days over calendar days, and summaries are weekly because weekend gaps dominated topical discontinuation [Carroll, 2004](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0190962204005547). PDC and MPR are pharmacy-refill constructs needing dispensing data and do not transfer to OTC cosmetics; topical "dose" is unstandardised, so logs capture frequency, not amount.

### Catalog versioning and claims boundary

GS1's rule: a formulation change that alters legally declared packaging information and that the brand expects consumers to distinguish requires a new GTIN [GS1 GTIN rule 263](https://www.gs1.org/1/gtinrules/en/rule/263/declared-formulation-or-functionality); that two-condition test separates a new ProductVersion from a data revision. Ingredients are keyed on INCI names with CAS/ChEBI secondary identifiers, as the TOXIN cosmetic-ingredient knowledge graph does [Sepehri, 2025](https://academic.oup.com/database/article/doi/10.1093/database/baae121/7989333); product fields borrow schema.org Product names (gtin, isVariantOf, additionalProperty) [schema.org Product](https://schema.org/Product). A relational store suffices; no ontology exists for consumer skincare concerns and none is needed.

Claims are governed by a whitelist. Cosmetic language is appearance language shared by the US and Indian definitions ("cleansing, beautifying, promoting attractiveness, or altering the appearance"); "treat acne" and "affect melanin production" are drug claims [FDA cosmetic vs drug](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/it-cosmetic-drug-or-both-or-it-soap); [CDSCO Cosmetics FAQ](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/cosmetics/Frequently-Asked-Questions-FAQcosmeticia.pdf). The Drugs and Magic Remedies Act criminalises advertising cures for scheduled conditions including leucoderma [DMRA 1954 summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drugs_and_Magic_Remedies_(Objectionable_Advertisements)_Act,_1954) (secondary source). EU 655/2013 requires evidence proportionate to the claim and forbids extrapolating ingredient properties to the product without the ingredient "at an effective concentration" [EU 655/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2013/655), so ProductVersion carries concentration bands and ClaimEvidence carries a level (product RCT → product instrumental test → consumer perception → ingredient-level plus concentration → formulation rationale only, not renderable). Testimonials are never a rationale: "anecdotal evidence… [is] never sufficient" and implied claims are the marketer's responsibility [FTC, 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance).

### Red-flag triage interlock

The interlock is a pre-filter that short-circuits the recommender. Triggers: nodulo-cystic acne or conglobata, scarring, persistent pigmentary change, non-response after two completed courses, persistent psychological distress, suspected fulminans (same-day) [NICE NG198 evidence review D](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng198/evidence/d-referral-to-specialist-care-pdf-9144159952); hormonal signs and irregular cycles [PRACT-India, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12382837/); ABCDE lesion descriptors in text or image flags [AAD ABCDEs](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/find/at-risk/abcdes); hot, swollen, painful skin or mood impact [NHS Acne](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/acne/). On trigger the system shows referral wording, offers at most a non-active base routine (cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen), stops scoring the affected region and records a RedFlagEvent; it never scores lesion risk, which FDA names as a device function [FDA MMA, 2022](https://www.fda.gov/media/80958/download).

## 8. Digital Twin / User Model

Drop the term "digital twin" externally and internally; the persistent artefact is a longitudinal skin-state model (a digital shadow) whose observation layer is an image-derived skin phenotype, and it must store provenance per fact and exclude raw faces, identity embeddings, disease labels and unconfirmed LLM inferences.

### Verdict on the term

NASEM requires a twin to be "dynamically updated with data from its physical twin," to have "a predictive capability," to inform decisions, and to run continual verification, validation and uncertainty quantification [NASEM, 2023](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK605502/); the Digital Twin Consortium adds that synchronisation frequency and fidelity must be stated [Digital Twin Consortium, 2020](https://www.digitaltwinconsortium.org/2020/12/digital-twin-consortium-defines-digital-twin/). The proposed system updates from voluntary selfies, has no validated prediction, and closes no verified loop; by Tudor's taxonomy it is a digital shadow, the category of 9.4% of reviewed studies, while only 12.08% met the twin criteria [Tudor, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01910-w). Health digital twins as described by Venkatesh et al. rely on deep phenotyping, population data and closed-loop optimisation under device-grade regulatory expectations [Venkatesh, 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-022-00694-7); none of that transfers. Using "twin" in marketing would also read as a performance claim under FTC net-impression rules [FTC, 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance).

Design choice on naming: externally "your skin timeline" or "skin profile"; internally "longitudinal skin-state model," with "skin phenotype" reserved for the per-scan CV outputs, a usage established in dermato-epidemiology for exactly these image-derived features [Hamer, 2020](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/126158/proefschrift-facial-skin-aging-merel-hamer.pdf). "Digital phenotyping" is defensible for the scan pipeline only; the canonical definition is "moment-by-moment, in situ quantification" from personal devices, which sparse active selfies plus self-report are not [Huckvale, 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-019-0166-1).

### What the persistent representation contains

Four layers: the append-only observation log with an Open mHealth-style envelope per data point; PROV-style Entity/Activity/Agent provenance rows [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/); derived current beliefs per attribute with confidence and justification, recomputable from the log so the engine can be re-run on history; and a small domain layer (concerns ↔ ingredient classes ↔ product versions ↔ routine slots) kept separate from personal data, in the spirit of personal health knowledge graphs but without clinical knowledge-base linkage [Rastogi & Zaki, 2020](http://www.cs.rpi.edu/~zaki/PaperDir/PHKG20.pdf).

| Provenance tier | Examples | May drive recommendations or displays? |
|---|---|---|
| Sensed | CV outputs, QualityReport, capture metadata | Yes, with uncertainty and version |
| Self-reported | Routine edits, use events, cycle and stress check-ins, Monk band at onboarding | Yes, labelled "you told us" |
| Inferred | Facts an LLM extracts from chat (skin type, sensitivities, habits) | No; quarantined as UserAssertion until confirmed |
| Confirmed | Inferred facts the user has explicitly confirmed | Yes, labelled "you confirmed" |

A "model of me" screen listing stored assumptions with their justifications is a classical user-modelling service [Kobsa, 2001](https://ics.uci.edu/~kobsa/papers/2001-UMUAI-kobsa.pdf) and satisfies the human-AI guidelines on explaining why the system did what it did and offering global controls [Amershi, 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf).

### What must not be stored

Raw face images beyond processing need, unless the user opted into the separate "retain originals for re-processing" tier with its own retention clock; by default keep measurements plus tightly cropped, EXIF-stripped region tiles [WP192, 2012](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/article-29/documentation/opinion-recommendation/files/2012/wp192_en.pdf). Identity embeddings or face matching across scans or users: biometric data becomes special-category "from the moment you collect" it when the purpose is unique identification [ICO biometric guidance, 2024](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/biometric-data-guidance-biometric-recognition/biometric-recognition/), and persisted face geometry is a BIPA "scan of… face geometry" with a three-year destruction clock [BIPA](https://law.justia.com/codes/illinois/chapter-740/act-740-ilcs-14/); continuity comes from authenticated accounts. Disease-labelled inferences (acne grade, melasma, rosacea, eczema, lesion risk), because the inference itself creates health data [WP29 Annex, 2015](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/article-29/documentation/other-document/files/2015/20150205_letter_art29wp_ec_health_data_after_plenary_annex_en.pdf) and is a device-type function. LLM-extracted facts as truth, because every mature memory design (MemGPT, Letta, Vertex Memory Bank) has the model write its own memory and inherits hallucination risk [MemGPT, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560); [Vertex Memory Bank, 2026-10-01](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/agent-engine/memory-bank/overview). Also excluded: race or ethnicity derived from skin tone (prohibited biometric categorisation [EU AI Act Art. 5](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/)), emotion, precise location, and third-party enrichment.

## 9. Gemini Architecture

Gemini should receive a compact, deterministically rendered per-user fact package and return schema-constrained text in which every claim cites a fact identifier, with numbers templated by the backend, a post-hoc grounding check, no Search grounding, paid tier only, and all memory writes quarantined as unconfirmed assertions.

### What Gemini knows, must not know, and how trusted state is supplied

Gemini knows exactly the ContextPackage: current per-region state with intervals, trend summary with evidence tier and alternative explanations, routine and adherence summary, active recommendations with fired rules and allowed claim text, goals, and an explicit list of unknowns ("no comparable scan in 14 days", "serum usage unlogged for 6 days"). It must not know raw images by default, identities (prompts are keyed by an opaque token), disease labels, or anything outside the package; Google's guidance is to "include instructions and information in a prompt that the model needs… instead of assuming that the model has all of the required information" [Gemini prompting strategies, 2026-09-17](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/prompting-strategies).

Layout follows positional evidence: models use information at the beginning or end of context far better than the middle [Liu, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172), and Google advises putting the question at the end [Gemini long context, 2026-06-22](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/long-context). Design choice: cached stable prefix (system instruction, schema, few-shot examples, policy text, catalog claims) → fact package → recent turns → question last. Facts are rendered as structured fields and as short fact sentences, because NLI-style verifiers expect prose premises [Rashkin, 2021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.12870).

### Deterministic numbers and schema-forced output

Every statistic is pre-computed server-side and quoted verbatim; the model performs no arithmetic, the same point as Toolformer's calculator delegation [Schick, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761) and Google's advice to enable code execution "whenever the model may need" counting. Structured output supports enum, required, additionalProperties, anyOf and $ref (doc updated 2026-09-23) [Gemini structured outputs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output), enough to force a claims array where each claim carries a state reference from an enum populated per request and a claim type from an allowed taxonomy (descriptive, routine/adherence, usage-as-directed, goal progress; never diagnosis, causal attribution, efficacy, dosage or "medical grade" comparison). Google still recommends "application-level validation… despite syntactically correct JSON output," so the backend rejects unknown references and string-matches every quoted number.

### Function calling only for long-tail retrieval

The default is one call per turn from the package; function calling is a second tier for questions needing data outside it (a metric's history over a window, a product's full record), using tool_choice "any" and allowed_tools to force a specific function [Gemini function calling](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/function-calling). Open-ended agent loops stay out of production: agents fit "open-ended problems where it's difficult or impossible to predict the required number of steps" and bring "higher costs and compounding error risks" [Anthropic, Building effective agents, 2024](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents); recall degrades as tokens grow ("context rot"), so the smallest high-signal set wins [Anthropic, Effective context engineering, 2025](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents).

### Post-verification

Layered defence: schema and reference validation; exact number matching; an NLI grounding check with a threshold below which the answer is regenerated or replaced by a templated fallback; offline evaluation. Two verifiers fit a small team: Google's Check Grounding API (answer ≤4,096 tokens, up to 200 facts, claim-level scores, latency under 500 ms; doc 2026-09-30) [Check Grounding](https://docs.cloud.google.com/generative-ai-app-builder/docs/check-grounding), which needs a GCP footprint, or the self-hostable Vectara HHEM-2.1-Open (0.1B parameters, under 600 MB RAM, about 1.5 s per 2k tokens on CPU, balanced accuracy 76.55% on AggreFact-SOTA and 74.28% on RAGTruth-QA) [HHEM-2.1-Open](https://huggingface.co/vectara/hallucination_evaluation_model). Neither is reliable per instance: automatic AIS correlated 0.96 with humans at system level but weakly per instance [Bohnet, 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.08037), so the fallback UX is budgeted. SelfCheckGPT detects inconsistency, not grounding, and is not a gate [Manakul, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08896).

### Memory, red flags and consent

Memory is two stores with different trust: the measured and logged user model, which the LLM never writes, and chat-derived facts the model may propose through a memory-candidates field with the quoted utterance, stored as unconfirmed UserAssertions and rendered only after confirmation. A session-end summary is ephemeral context, never promoted into the user model. Red flags are handled by a deterministic pre-classifier that bypasses generation and returns fixed referral wording, mirroring HealthBench's "emergency referrals" and "responding under uncertainty" themes [HealthBench, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08775).

For Indian users, sending chat context to Google is a cross-border transfer lawful under DPDP's blacklist model, but it must appear in the consent notice as its own purpose (the "share my skin history with the AI assistant" tier) and erasure must fan out to any Google-side state [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf); the Rules notified 14 November 2025 add a 72-hour Board breach notice and a 90-day rights-request window [DPDP Rules PIB release, 2025](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf). The brand logs its own pseudonymised prompt-template version, rule-set version, package hash and response in-region, because Google's 55-day abuse log is not available to it. The EU AI Act chatbot-disclosure duty applies from 2 August 2026 [EU AI Act Art. 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/) (secondary source for dates).

## 10. Dashboard Information Architecture

The dashboard is a set of answers to five user questions, each shown with its evidence tier and uncertainty in categorical form with a number alongside, and each with an explicit list of what the product refuses to show.

| User question | What answers it | Evidence tier / uncertainty shown | Must NOT be shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| What changed since last time? | Side-by-side images with the comparability verdict per region; per-region deltas in absolute units only when the pair is comparable and |Δ| > MDC95 | Verdict (comparable / with caveats / not comparable) with the named caveat; "scan-to-scan noise for this metric is ±X" | Percent change on near-zero baselines; any delta from a non-comparable pair; whole-face composite scores |
| What is improving, what is stable, what is worsening? | Per-metric status from the gate sequence: baseline forming / within noise / observation / pattern, with direction and the baseline window used | Tier 0–2 label; number of comparable scans and weeks ("8 scans, 6 weeks, 5 scheduled"); gaps shown as uncertainty, not interpolated lines | Trend labels below 4 points; the five-point trend rule; percentile or "skin age" scores; disease grades |
| Is my routine doing anything? | Timeline overlay of product start/stop and adherence coverage against the metric; Tier 3 "observed alongside" text with concurrent changes enumerated (cycle, season, settling, other products) | Tier 3 only when temporality, implementation ≥ ~70% and coverage ≥ 50% hold; "too early" inside the active's expected window | "Product X improved your skin"; efficacy percentages; any statement when ≥2 products started in the same 4-week window |
| Am I using it as planned? | Weekly adherence summary per product: used / skipped / unlogged counts, implementation ratio over logged days, persistence | Coverage stated with the ratio ("used on 9 of 12 logged days; 16 days unlogged") | Adherence percentages over calendar days that treat unlogged as missed; streak shaming |
| What should I do next? | Current recommendations with the rule that fired and its knowledge source; routine with slots, ramp stage and warnings; red-flag hand-off when triggered | Explanation of the rule (which user fact × which product attribute); claim text from ClaimEvidence with source and date | Treatment language; disease names; comparison to dermatologist accuracy; forecasts of outcome |
| How does my skin compare to others? | Only once population data exist: Indian reference context in frequency form ("most people your age in our reference data scored 2–3") | Reference-set description (n, device, conditions) | Percentile ranks; "skin age"; any cross-user benchmark before in-population data |

Confidence is categorical first because numeric confidence "assumes users understand probability" and should be omitted if it "doesn't make an impact on user decision making" [PAIR Explainability + Trust](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/), but a number accompanies each verbal label because verbal terms alone show "substantial variability between individuals in their interpretation" [van der Bles, 2019](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsos.181870). Indirect uncertainty (scans, weeks, lighting variation) is stated before any range, frequencies ("3 in 10") are preferred to percentages or "1-in-x" framings [Spiegelhalter, 2017](https://www.annualreviews.org/doi/10.1146/annurev-statistics-010814-020148), and any mobile range uses discrete quantile dotplots rather than density plots [Kay, 2016](https://idl.uw.edu/papers/when-ish-is-my-bus). A "how this works and what it cannot do" surface plus notifications when the engine or prompt version changes follow the transparency principles for machine-learning-enabled devices, adopted voluntarily [Transparency principles, 2024](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/transparency-machine-learning-enabled-medical-devices-guiding-principles).

## 11. Example User Journey

The journey shows the system saying less than the user wants at two weeks and earning the right to say more by six weeks, without ever attributing an outcome to the product.

**User.** Adult, 27, India, self-reported Monk band 7, goal "uneven tone and marks on cheeks," pinned to the front camera, scheduled anchor scan every Sunday morning. Routine accepted from the rule set: syndet cleanser AM/PM, a 5% niacinamide serum PM (ramp: nightly from the start, as no irritant active is involved), non-comedogenic sunscreen AM (non-removable item). No red-flag triggers at intake.

| Scan | Day | Type | Quality / comparability | What the system records |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 0 | Scheduled | Adequate; baseline | Per-region pigmentation area × darkness, lesion counts, CIELAB per region; MDC95 attached per metric from the engine's calibration table; "baseline forming" |
| S2 | 7 | Scheduled | Adequate; comparable to S1 | All region deltas within MDC95; status "within noise"; still "baseline forming" (fewer than 4 points) |
| S3 | 14 | Ad hoc, 23:10, warm overhead light, flash fired | Pass with caveats for lesion counts; **not comparable** for colour metrics (flash state and illumination differ from the series) | Lesion counts recorded; colour deltas suppressed; retake offered; capture conditions stored |
| S4 | 28 | Scheduled | Adequate; comparable | Left-malar darkness delta vs baseline window exceeds MDC95; right malar within noise; 3 comparable points → direction only |
| S5 | 42 | Scheduled | Adequate; comparable | Left-malar change persists across S4–S5; 4 comparable points → Tier 1 "observation"; persistence test not yet applicable (<10 points) |

**Adherence by day 16.** Sixteen calendar days since start: 13 days logged as used, 2 logged as skipped, 1 unlogged. Implementation 13 of 15 logged days (87%); coverage 15 of 16. Both exceed the Tier 3 gates, but the evidence tier is still 0 because the product's expected-effect window has not opened and only two comparable scans exist.

**Question on day 16:** "I've been using this product for two weeks and I don't see much improvement."

The ContextPackage contains: S1–S3 facts with comparability verdicts; within-noise deltas with their MDC95; the adherence summary; the niacinamide expected-effect window from the knowledge base; unknowns ("S3 colour not comparable: flash and lighting differed"; "no cycle information provided"). Gemini's schema-forced answer cites those facts in four parts:

1. **Observation.** "Across your two comparable scans (day 0 and day 7) the pigmentation scores on both cheeks moved less than the scan-to-scan noise for this measurement, so there is no measurable change yet. Your day-14 scan was taken under different lighting with flash, so its colour values were not compared."
2. **Association.** "You have used the serum on 13 of the 15 days you logged. That is enough usage data to relate to your scans later, but two comparable scans is not enough to describe a pattern."
3. **Plausible explanation.** "This is expected at two weeks: in an eight-week study of 5% niacinamide, a decrease in hyperpigmented area was first detectable at four weeks. The first weeks after you start tracking often settle on their own, and other things that change over a month (sun exposure, cycle timing, stress) also move these scores." The time-to-effect statement traces to the knowledge-base entry [Boo, 2021](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3921/10/8/1315); the settling statement to the regression-to-the-mean rule [Barnett, 2005](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/34/1/215/638499).
4. **What would give more evidence.** "Keep the Sunday morning scan in the same light without flash; two more scheduled scans will let the app report a direction, and about ten comparable scans are needed before it can call a change persistent. Keep logging, including skipped days."

The validator confirms every number matches the package; the NLI check scores each sentence against the fact sentences; no causal or efficacy claim type exists in the output schema, so "the serum is working" or "is not working" cannot be generated.

**By day 42.** The package carries a Tier 1 observation for left-malar darkness (delta beyond MDC95 on four comparable scans), unchanged right-malar status, implementation 83% over the window, and two concurrent changes recorded as ContextEvents (season boundary; one high-stress check-in). The same question now yields an observation with its absolute delta and noise band, a note that the change was "observed alongside" the routine with both concurrent changes listed, a statement that the trial-literature window for this active has opened, and the remaining requirement for a pattern (ten comparable points). At no point does the text state that the product caused the change, present a forecast, or use a disease label.

## 12. What Should NOT Be Built Yet

Every item below either lacks an evidentiary floor, violates a regulatory or contractual line, or would silently convert noise into claims.

| Do not build yet | Reason from the evidence |
|---|---|
| Individual skin-state forecasting | Observable covariates explain 22% (men) to 37.2% (women) of wrinkle variation with ~55% heritability [Hamer, 2020](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/126158/proefschrift-facial-skin-aging-merel-hamer.pdf); the only Indian reference set is cross-sectional and "cannot infer individual skin aging trajectories" [Reddy, 2026](https://www.cureus.com/articles/465674-artificial-intelligence-driven-skin-parameter-assessment-in-indian-adults-establishing-population-specific-reference-data-for-clinical-dermatology); unquantified uncertainty must not be surfaced as prediction [Sel, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01447-y); a forecast chart is an implied efficacy claim [FTC, 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance); "prediction… of any disease" is a medical purpose [CDSCO MDSW guidance, 2026](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Guidance-document-on-Medical-Device-Software-under-MDR-2017.pdf) |
| Individual causal attribution ("product X improved your skin") | N-of-1 inference needs stable conditions, rapid onset/offset, washout and randomised crossovers [AHRQ, 2014](https://effectivehealthcare.ahrq.gov/products/n-1-trials/research-2014-5); non-randomised evidence starts at low certainty [Cochrane ch. 14](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14); vehicle arms improved 19.9–40% [Iraji, 2007](https://ijdvl.com/efficacy-of-topical-azelaic-acid-gel-in-the-treatment-of-mild-moderate-acne-vulgaris/) |
| Collaborative filtering / "users like you" | Cold-start and sparsity with a small own catalog; no representation of contraindications [Felfernig et al.](https://www.ise.bgu.ac.il/faculty/liorr/recsyshb/chConstraint.pdf); [Burke, 2002](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1021240730564) |
| ML ranking before propensity logging | Off-policy evaluation needs logged contexts, actions and propensities from a stochastic policy [Saito, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07146) |
| LLM-written memory as truth | Memory is a model output in every mature design [MemGPT, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560); no published extraction accuracy for Memory Bank [Vertex Memory Bank](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/agent-engine/memory-bank/overview) |
| Face matching for identity or scan continuity | Converts the pipeline to Art. 9 biometric processing "from the moment you collect" [ICO, 2024](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/biometric-data-guidance-biometric-recognition/biometric-recognition/); BIPA/CUBI exposure [BIPA](https://law.justia.com/codes/illinois/chapter-740/act-740-ilcs-14/) |
| Percentile or "skin age" scores | VISIA percentile SD ≈ 9–11% vs absolute ≈ 3% [Henseler, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10665717/); normative data for Indian phone captures do not exist |
| Automatic diagnosis or disease grading | Device territory in all three jurisdictions [FDA MMA, 2022](https://www.fda.gov/media/80958/download); [MDCG 2019-11](https://health.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2020-09/md_mdcg_2019_11_guidance_en_0.pdf); [CDSCO MDSW guidance, 2026](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Guidance-document-on-Medical-Device-Software-under-MDR-2017.pdf); storing the inference creates health data [WP29, 2015](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/article-29/documentation/other-document/files/2015/20150205_letter_art29wp_ec_health_data_after_plenary_annex_en.pdf) |
| Cross-user benchmarks before population data | Reference curves from one imaging setup may not transfer to phones [Reddy, 2026](https://www.cureus.com/articles/465674-artificial-intelligence-driven-skin-parameter-assessment-in-indian-adults-establishing-population-specific-reference-data-for-clinical-dermatology); drift tests needed ~4,000 images for significance at a 40% shift [Kore, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46142-w) |
| ComBat-style or histogram harmonisation across sessions | ComBat needs many samples per known batch; a session is one sample with biology confounded with batch [Sci Rep ComBat, 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-23328-0); histogram matching deletes the pigment/redness change by construction (inference) |
| Visit-process weighting and mixed-effects shrinkage | Need many users to estimate between-person variance [Huque, 2018](https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12874-018-0615-6); [IrregLong vignette](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/IrregLong/vignettes/Irreglong-vignette.html) |
| Under-18 users in India | "Child" is under 18; verifiable parental consent and a ban on behavioural monitoring of children [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf); Gemini terms require 18+ [Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) |
| Generated "future face" imagery | Must be machine-readably marked synthetic [EU AI Act Art. 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/); GAN ageing studies are perception research, not validated trajectories |
| Agent loops and guardrail frameworks as runtime | Single schema-constrained call covers the bounded task; frameworks add runtimes and extra LLM calls [NeMo Guardrails](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/guardrails/latest/index.html); [Anthropic, 2024](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) |

## 13. Evaluation Framework

Evaluation must be split into eight independent tracks, because a system can score well on image-analysis accuracy while its longitudinal claims are noise, its explanations are satisfying but ineffective, and its chatbot is fluent but ungrounded.

| Track | What is measured | Protocol | Acceptance anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Image-analysis accuracy | Agreement of engine grades and counts with a 3-dermatologist panel on the section 5 scales; per Monk-bin breakdown | Cross-sectional validity study; weighted kappa model-vs-panel and panel-vs-panel; Bland–Altman for counts; multiple graders on dark-skin images because dermatologist sensitivity fell 0.72→0.59 on FST V–VI [Daneshjou, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9374341/) | Model-vs-panel agreement at or above panel-vs-panel is the ceiling; human ICC 0.74–0.93 on these scales [Lorenc, 2021](https://academic.oup.com/asjopenforum/article/3/4/ojab039/6407293) |
| Measurement reliability | ICC(2,1), SEM, MDC95, weighted kappa per metric × region × tone band × capture-quality bucket | Same-day repeated capture (≥30 users, ≥3 captures, two lighting conditions) plus a 4-week repeat session [Henseler, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10665717/); user-level splits [GMLP, 2021](https://www.fda.gov/media/153486/download) | ICC or weighted kappa ≥0.70 [COSMIN, 2024](https://www.cosmin.nl/wp-content/uploads/COSMIN-manual-V2_final.pdf); SEM in grade units against the one-grade convention |
| Longitudinal consistency | False-positive rate of observation/pattern labels on stable users; comparability-verdict agreement with humans; version-boundary handling | Re-run the comparison layer on the reliability cohort (true change ≈ 0) and on synthetic series with known shifts, using Anhøj's likelihood-ratio framing [Anhøj, 2015](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0121349) | Tier ≥1 labels on stable users ≤ the nominal 5%; no cross-version trends |
| Recommendation quality | Rule conformance, catalog coverage, routine-level complementarity; effectiveness vs satisfaction measured separately | Offline regression suite of synthetic user cases per rule (zero hard-constraint violations); holdout cohorts on adherence and irritation reports; propensity logging before any stochastic ranker [Saito, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07146) | Effectiveness and satisfaction are distinct aims; personalisation "was detrimental to effectiveness" in one study [Tintarev & Masthoff, 2012](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11257-011-9117-5) |
| Adherence accuracy | Validity of in-app logs; bias direction | Compare logs with product weight or reminder-confirmation in a small cohort; report method and definition per EMERGE [EMERGE](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/espacomp-medication-adherence-reporting-guideline-emerge/) | Expect logs to overstate use [Carroll, 2004](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0190962204005547); treat unlogged as unknown |
| LLM factual grounding | Fraction of claims supported by the package; number fidelity; refusal on disallowed claim types | Golden set of (frozen package, question, rubric); two-phase judging (eligibility, then groundedness) with ≥2 judge families to counter self-family bias [FACTS Grounding, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03200); atomic-claim scoring [FActScore, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251); RAGAS faithfulness with the HHEM variant in CI [RAGAS](https://docs.ragas.io/en/stable/concepts/metrics/available_metrics/faithfulness/); human slice annotated with AIS two-stage guidelines [Rashkin, 2021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.12870) | Frontier models reached ~84% fully grounded on 32k-token documents; packages are smaller, so higher is plausible but must be measured; worst-of-n on the safety subset [HealthBench, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08775) |

Reporting anchors: write the engine validation report to TRIPOD+AI (subgroup performance, calibration, missing-data handling, development-vs-evaluation differences) [TRIPOD+AI, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11019967/), use CLAIM for the imaging-model description and STARD-AI for any concern-detection accuracy study [EQUATOR AI/ML index](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines-study-design/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning-studies/), and follow GMLP's independence, representativeness and monitoring principles [GMLP, 2021](https://www.fda.gov/media/153486/download). A model card per engine and prompt version and an intended-use statement mirroring the wellness definitions are cheap, protective artefacts.

Drift monitoring is label-free and sequential: log embedding features and per-metric output distributions by device model, OS, app version and Monk bin, and run maximum-mean-discrepancy tests on rolling windows, because image- and output-based detection caught drift that aggregate performance monitoring missed [Kore, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46142-w); frame alarms as control charts with anytime-valid false-discovery control across metrics and subgroups [Dolin, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05701v3). Design choice: a quarterly labelled audit of about 200 images re-anchors concept drift, and the monitor must separate population drift (new users) from within-user change (the product's signal).

## 14. Major Risks

| Risk | Type | How the architecture reduces it |
|---|---|---|
| Capture artefacts reported as skin change (ΔE 10–26 device/lighting error vs 2–6% rig noise) | Scientific | Capture gates, comparability verdict per region, same-camera/flash pinning, MDC95 floor before any delta is shown |
| Engine outputs for redness, sebum, hydration are proxies without validity | Scientific | Demote to labelled proxies or omit; low-confidence tier for redness; proxy label carries its own validation status |
| Noise or regression to the mean labelled as "improving" | Scientific | Baseline window, RCI/MDC gate, ≥10-point persistence rule, "too early" tier inside expected-effect windows |
| Causal or efficacy language reaching users | Product / regulatory | Claim-type taxonomy in the output schema; whitelist scan; evidence tier caps at "observed alongside"; testimonials excluded from rationale |
| Product drifts into device territory (disease labels, lesion risk, alerts) | Health / regulatory | No Condition entity; red-flag interlock refers without scoring; intended-use statement; FDA/MDCG/CDSCO wellness definitions as the design boundary |
| Hallucinated numbers or facts in chat | Technical | Numbers pre-templated; state-reference enum; backend string match; NLI post-check with templated fallback; golden-set evaluation |
| LLM-written memory corrupts the user model | Technical / privacy | UserAssertion quarantine until confirmed; LLM has no write path to measured state |
| Engine upgrade silently breaks longitudinal comparisons | Technical | Engine version on every SkinState; within-version trends; re-processing of retained originals under consent; version boundary shown |
| Face data becomes biometric or health special-category data | Privacy | No face matching; landmarks transient; crops not full frames; explicit per-tier consent; skin tone used only as a photometric covariate |
| DPDP erasure, breach and child obligations | Privacy / legal | User-level deletion fan-out including Google-side state; 72-hour breach runbook; 18+ gate; separate consent for AI-assistant sharing |
| Informative sampling biases every trend | Scientific | Scheduled anchor scans; scheduled/ad hoc tags; coverage shown; pessimistic-missingness check before "improvement" messages |
| Self-reported adherence overstates use | Scientific / product | Unlogged = unknown; implementation over logged days with coverage; weekly summaries; Tier 3 gated on coverage |
| Skin-tone performance gap on the core user base | Scientific / fairness | Monk/ITA-stratified calibration and evaluation powered within MST 4–9; per-bin error in the model card; block attributes whose per-bin error exceeds the noise floor |
| Over-trust and automation bias in lay users | Product | Categorical confidence with numbers alongside; indirect uncertainty first; "what it cannot do" surface; change notifications on engine/prompt updates |
| Vendor dependency on Gemini terms and retention | Technical / privacy | Paid tier; store=false or own history store; no Search grounding; own in-region prompt log; Bedrock in ap-south-1 as a documented fallback |
| Pipeline duplicates or lost events | Technical | Idempotent consumers keyed by scan identifier; Standard workflow exactly-once start; DLQs on every rule; append-only log as the durable record |

## 15. Simplified Final Architecture

Nine CORE elements make the product credible; four OPTIONAL elements add value when the user base or catalog justifies them; five LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT elements need data the team does not yet have.

| Tier | Element | Why it sits here |
|---|---|---|
| CORE | Guided capture with on-device pose/expression/exposure/distance/blur gates and a named-reason retake loop | Comparability is engineered at capture; clinical standards offer no post-hoc rescue |
| CORE | Ingest pipeline: presigned upload, malware scan, re-encode with EXIF to sidecar, idempotent Standard workflow per scan | Security baseline and at-least-once delivery handling |
| CORE | Existing CV engine re-specified to region-wise ordinal grades, counts and CIELAB with engine version and quality flags | The only outputs that survive the move to phone photos |
| CORE | Test–retest noise model (SEM/MDC95 tables by metric × region × tone band) produced by the team | No published values exist; every downstream claim depends on it |
| CORE | Append-only event log in PostgreSQL (ap-south-1) with provenance tiers and recomputable projections | Preserves the explanatory timeline; supports erasure at user level |
| CORE | Comparability verdict and gate sequence (noise → importance → persistence) emitting evidence tiers 0–3 with alternative explanations | Converts noisy sparse series into honest statements |
| CORE | Constraint-based recommender over a GTIN-versioned catalog: decision tables, red-flag interlock, fired-rule audit, claims whitelist | Deterministic, explainable, compliant |
| CORE | Context package → Gemini single schema-forced call → validator → NLI check → fallback; paid tier; UserAssertion quarantine | LLM renders, never computes or writes |

| OPTIONAL | Scheduled anchor scans with fixed-time reminders and cycle/stress/season context events | Improves sampling and alternative-explanation logic; depends on user tolerance |
| OPTIONAL | Function-calling tier for long-tail chat questions and pgvector retrieval over the ingredient corpus | Only when package-answerable turns prove insufficient |
| OPTIONAL | Opt-in on/off protocol (Tier 4) for fast-onset/offset products only | Bounded by N-of-1 criteria; small user segment |
| LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT | Population priors, mixed-effects shrinkage, visit-process weighting | Need many users and in-population longitudinal data |
| LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT | ML ranking with off-policy evaluation | Needs propensity logs and interaction density |
| LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT | Cross-user benchmarks and Indian reference context | Needs phone-captured, tone-stratified reference data |
| LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT | Any forecast (population-conditioned scenario with quantified uncertainty, never an outcome) | Needs calibration study, longitudinal cohort, written context-of-use |
| LATER-RESEARCH-DEPENDENT | "Digital twin" naming | Requires validated prediction, closed loop and continual VVUQ |

### Direct answer

The simplest technically credible architecture keeps the existing engine and chatbot and wraps them in a measurement discipline: a guided-capture gate, an engine-versioned append-only skin-state log, a noise-aware comparison layer that outputs evidence tiers, a deterministic rule set over a versioned catalog, and a fact package that Gemini only narrates. The decisions that must be gotten right are:

1. Run the test–retest study on the existing engine and ship MDC95 tables before any change claim is shown.
2. Re-specify engine outputs as per-region ordinal grades, counts and CIELAB with engine version; demote redness, sebum and hydration to labelled proxies or remove them.
3. Make capture control and a three-tier comparability verdict the first gate; never normalise sessions post hoc.
4. Store everything append-only with provenance tiers, and draw trends only within an engine version.
5. Replace percent change with absolute deltas against a baseline window, gated by RCI/MDC and a ≥10-point persistence rule.
6. Build recommendation as decision tables with hit policies, a red-flag interlock and a fired-rule audit; encode only sourced rules.
7. Give Gemini a deterministic fact package with pre-templated numbers, force schema output with state references, and verify post hoc; paid tier, no Search grounding, 18+.
8. Quarantine every chat-derived fact until the user confirms it.
9. Stratify calibration, evaluation and the model card by Monk/ITA bands powered on the Indian user base.
10. Design consent tiers, retention and user-level deletion fan-out before collecting the first longitudinal series.
